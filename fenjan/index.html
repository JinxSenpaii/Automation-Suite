How it works

The AI only reads what the customer means. Items, prices, totals, opening hours and delivery fees always come from the sheet and fixed rules, so the bot can't invent a price.

1

A DM arrives

Messenger and Instagram send each new customer message to n8n. Echoes, reactions and read receipts are skipped.

2

Gemini reads it

It returns JSON: intent, items by menu ID, pickup or delivery, address and language. Arabic, English and Franco all work.

3

Rules build the reply

The cart is updated from the Menu tab, totals are calculated, and the bot asks only for what is missing: pickup or delivery, the address, then "confirm".

4

The kitchen takes over

A confirmed order lands in Orders and on Telegram. Each status the kitchen sets messages the customer in their own language.

Built with

  • n8n (self-hosted)
  • Meta Graph API: Messenger, Instagram, Facebook Page
  • Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite
  • Google Sheets: Menu, Carts, Orders, Specials, Errors
  • Telegram for the kitchen and alerts

Safeguards

  • Unknown or unavailable items are named, never added
  • Max 20 of one item; delivery needs an address
  • Orders are only confirmed 8:00 to midnight
  • Unreadable messages go to a person
  • Every failure is logged and sent to Telegram

Run it yourself

Import both workflow JSON files, create the 5-tab Google Sheet (Menu, Carts, Orders, Specials, Errors), connect Meta, Google Sheets, Gemini and Telegram credentials, test locally with Sample DM, then activate Fenjan 2 and Fenjan 1.
