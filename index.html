DAWAA 2 · EXPIRY, STOCK & ERRORS

Back office

Three background jobs keep the shelf honest. Reservations not picked up in 2 hours expire and their stock goes back. Every night at 22:00, low stock becomes a reorder list. Any failure is classified and sent to the team.

Demo clock 13:30

Every 15 minutes: expiry sweep

Finds reserved lines past their 2-hour hold, marks them expired, returns the stock and tells the customer.

Reserve something at the counter, advance the clock 2 hours, then run the sweep.

Every day 22:00: reorder list

Every medicine at or below its reorder level goes to the pharmacists on Telegram and to purchasing by email.

Tip: Brufen (8/10) and Cidophage (0/10) are already below reorder level, or edit any stock number below.

On any error: classify & alert

Every failure becomes one row: which workflow, which node, what kind of error. Critical ones are also emailed.

Pick a failure above and click Trigger error.
